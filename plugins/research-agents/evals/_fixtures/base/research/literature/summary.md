# Literature summary

This summary records the conclusion approved at G2 (D-002). Per-paper notes and a bibliography were not kept for this project, so no work is cited here.

- Curriculum ordering is reported to speed up training in some settings and not in others; the reported effects depend on model size and task.
- Within the searched sources, no directly equivalent controlled comparison was identified for 2-layer transformers on multi-digit addition.
- Any novelty claim must stay restricted to this model size and task (reviewer-2, D-002).
