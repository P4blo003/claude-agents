# Research Brief

Status: APPROVED — D-001, 2026-10-02
Last updated: 2026-10-16

## Research question

Does curriculum ordering (short-to-long sequences) reduce the number of training steps a small transformer needs to reach 95% exact match on multi-digit addition?

## Motivation

Curriculum learning is often reported to speed up training, but evidence for small models on algorithmic tasks is mixed. A clean, controlled comparison would settle it for this setting.

## Discipline and research type

- Discipline / subfield: Machine learning / training dynamics
- Research type(s): computational, experimental

## Scope

- In scope: 2-layer transformers, addition of up to 8-digit numbers
- Out of scope: other arithmetic operations, models above 10M parameters

## Initial hypotheses or propositions

H1: curriculum ordering reaches 95% exact match in fewer training steps than random ordering. Refuted if the median steps-to-threshold across seeds is not lower.

## Constraints

- Data, participants or sources (availability, confidentiality, licences, ethics approval): synthetic data generated by script; no ethics approval needed
- Resources (compute, equipment, budget): one GPU, 20 GPU-hours
- Time (deadline): 2026-12-15
