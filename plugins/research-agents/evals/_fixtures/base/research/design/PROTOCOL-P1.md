# Protocol P1 — Curriculum ordering for multi-digit addition

Status: APPROVED — D-003, 2026-10-16
Version: v1
Related decisions: D-003
Research type: computational experiment
Applicable reporting guideline / standard: the target venue's ML reproducibility checklist

## 1. Question, hypotheses or propositions

- H1: Curriculum ordering (short-to-long) reaches 95% exact match on the evaluation set in fewer training steps than random ordering — Expected: lower median steps-to-threshold — Refuted if: the median is not lower (one-sided test at α = 0.05).

## 2. Research type and justification

Controlled computational experiment. Alternative considered: comparing published training runs (rejected: budgets and hyperparameters differ across papers).

## 3. Data, sources or participants

| Source / sample | Provenance | Size | Inclusion / exclusion | Licence / confidentiality | Why included |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic addition problems, up to 8 digits | generated by src/curriculum/data.py | 200k train, 10k eval | eval generated with seed 999; problems also in train removed by hash | N/A (synthetic) | Task under study |

- Sampling strategy and sample-size / power justification: 10 seeds per condition; pilot variance gives 80% power for a 25% reduction in median steps.

## 4. Procedure

Train a 2-layer, 4-head transformer (d = 128) for 20k steps per run with the pilot's hyperparameters. Evaluate exact match on the evaluation set every 100 steps.

## 5. Comparison conditions

| Condition / baseline / control | Why included | Budget or conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Random ordering | Simplest reasonable reference | 20k steps, same hyperparameters |
| Curriculum ordering (short-to-long) | Condition under test | 20k steps, same hyperparameters |

## 6. Measures

- Primary outcome or metric (fixed now): steps to reach 95% exact match on the evaluation set
- Secondary measures: final exact match at 20k steps (in-distribution)
- Validity for the intended construct: steps-to-threshold measures sample efficiency directly.

## 7. Bias and leakage control

Evaluation set generated with a separate seed and checked against training by hash. Hyperparameters fixed from the pilot and never tuned on the evaluation set.

## 8. Analysis plan

- Confirmatory analyses: (1) H1 — one-sided Mann–Whitney U on steps-to-threshold, α = 0.05, with rank-biserial effect size; (2) final exact match — two-sided Mann–Whitney U.
- Exploratory analyses (labelled as such): exact match on 9- and 10-digit addition after 20k steps (length generalization).
- Assumption checks and pre-specified fallbacks (what each confirmatory analysis uses if its assumptions fail): a run that never reaches the threshold is assigned 20k steps, and a permutation test on medians replaces the Mann–Whitney U.
- Quantitative: unit of analysis = training run (seed); median and IQR reported.
- Qualitative: N/A — computational study.

## 9. Ablations or sensitivity analyses

N/A — not planned for this workshop paper.

## 10. Tuning and selection (computational work only)

No tuning: hyperparameters fixed from the pilot.

## 11. Reproducibility plan

Configs, seeds, commit, environment lockfile and raw evaluation logs saved for every run.

## 12. Ethics and data management

- At least 10 seeds per condition; report median and IQR.
