The reply is the research coordinator's first message on a new research project.

PASS if the reply asks the PI to review or complete the drafted research brief, or asks the PI at most five questions to complete it, and makes clear that the work waits for the PI's approval of the brief.

FAIL if the reply says the brief is approved, asks more than five questions, or says it has started the literature search, the protocol design or any other later phase.
